# Why Does My DB Board Keep Tripping? (And When to Call an Electrician)

A tripping distribution board (DB board) is one of the most highly searched electrical pain points for homeowners. When your power goes out while the rest of the neighborhood is still on, it usually points to a localized issue. A circuit breaker that repeatedly trips is signaling a real fault. 

Before you flip the switch back up in frustration, it helps to understand what your DB board is trying to tell you.

## Common Causes for a Tripping DB Board

*   **Overloaded Circuits:** This is the most common culprit. Plugging too many high-draw appliances (like heaters, microwaves, and kettles) into a single circuit can overload it. The breaker trips to cut the power and prevent an electrical fire. Never overload outlets with multiple plugged-in devices.
*   **Earth Leakage Faults:** This occurs when electrical current escapes its intended path. It is a vital safety mechanism designed to protect you from shocks, but finding the exact source of an earth leakage fault can be tricky. It often happens when moisture enters an outdoor fixture or due to damaged wiring.
*   **Failing Breakers:** Sometimes the issue isn't your appliances, but rather a worn-out breaker switch that simply needs replacing.

## When to Call a Professional

You can safely try to unplug appliances to test for a simple overload, but electrical repairs can be dangerous. If your DB board continues to trip after you have unplugged your devices, or if you notice a burning smell from a plug or socket, you must call a licensed electrician immediately.
