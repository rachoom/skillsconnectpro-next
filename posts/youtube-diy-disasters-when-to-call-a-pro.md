It usually happens on a Saturday morning. You notice a dripping tap or a loose cabinet door. Instead of calling a professional, you pull out your phone, open YouTube, and watch a cheerful guy named "Toolbox Tim" fix it in exactly four minutes.

"I can do that," you tell yourself. 

Three hours, two trips to the hardware store, and R800 worth of tools later, your kitchen is flooded, the dog is hiding, and you are desperately trying to seal a pipe with duct tape and prayers. 

We love the South African "Maak 'n Plan" spirit. But there is a fine line between being resourceful and accidentally launching a costly home demolition. Here are three common DIY traps where you should immediately put the phone down and call a verified pro.

### 1. The "It’s Just a Little Leak" Plumbing Incident
Water respects no man, and it certainly doesn't respect the wrench you bought on special. What starts as an attempt to replace a simple washer often ends with a stripped thread and a miniature geyser in your bathroom. 
**The Reality:** Plumbers don't just tighten pipes; they understand water pressure, hidden seals, and drainage gradients. If it involves the main water line, step away from the toolbox. 

### 2. The "I Think This Wire is Dead" Shocking Revelation
Electrical DIY is the only home improvement project that can literally bite you back. If you are watching a video titled "How to bypass a tripping DB board," you are not fixing a problem—you are creating a fire hazard. 
**The Reality:** Electricians spend years studying loads, phases, and compliance for a reason. If your "fix" involves twisting two wires together and wrapping them in electrical tape, you need a certified sparky, not a tutorial.

### 3. The "A Bit of Polyfilla Will Hide That" Structural Fix
Got a massive, zig-zagging crack running up your exterior wall? Shoving a tube of crack-filler into it and painting it over is like putting a plaster on a broken leg. 
**The Reality:** Structural cracks usually point to foundation movement or water damage. A professional builder will diagnose *why* the wall is cracking before they fix it, ensuring your house stays standing for the next decade.
