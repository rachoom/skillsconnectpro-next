# 5 Red Flags to Watch Out For When Hiring a Local Handyman

Finding the right person for a home repair or renovation project can be stressful. We've all heard the horror stories of disappearing deposits and half-finished jobs. While most artisans are hardworking professionals, it pays to be vigilant.

If you are currently sourcing quotes for a project, keep an eye out for these five critical red flags.

## 1. They Demand a Large Upfront Cash Deposit
While it's standard for a contractor to request a deposit to cover initial material costs (usually 20% to 30%), a demand for a massive cash upfront payment—especially 50% or more—is a major warning sign. Legitimate professionals have enough cash flow or trade credit to start a project without needing you to bankroll the entire operation on day one.

## 2. Refusal to Put Things in Writing
A verbal agreement is a recipe for disaster. If an artisan is hesitant to provide a written quote, a detailed scope of work, or a clear contract, walk away. A professional contract protects both you and the contractor, outlining exactly what will be done, the timeline, and the payment schedule.

## 3. Unusually Low Bids
If you receive three quotes and one is drastically lower than the others, be cautious. Desperate contractors may underbid to win the job, only to hit you with "unforeseen" costs later, or worse, cut corners on materials and safety to make a profit.

## 4. No Verifiable References or Reviews
In today's digital age, an established artisan should have a trail of satisfied customers. If they cannot provide references, or if they have no online footprint or reviews, it's a gamble. 

## 5. They Pressure You to Decide Immediately
High-pressure sales tactics have no place in home improvement. "Today only" discounts or warnings that their schedule will instantly fill up are designed to make you act on emotion rather than logic. Take your time to review the quote and check their credentials.
