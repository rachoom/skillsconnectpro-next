# What to Do When a Pipe Bursts: Immediate Steps Before the Plumber Arrives

A burst pipe is a homeowner's worst nightmare. The sound of rushing water and the immediate threat of flooding can cause panic. However, acting quickly and methodically in those first few minutes can save you thousands in water damage and structural repairs. 

While you wait for a verified plumbing professional from Skills Connect Pro to arrive, here is your emergency action plan.

## 1. Shut Off the Main Water Supply Immediately
Do not wait to assess the damage. Find your home's main water shut-off valve and turn it off completely. 
*   **Where is it?** This is typically located near your water meter, on the boundary line of your property, or sometimes in the garage or basement. 
*   **Tip:** If you don't know where yours is right now, find it today. Don't wait for an emergency.

## 2. Turn Off the Electricity
Water and electricity are a deadly combination. If the burst pipe is leaking near electrical outlets, light fixtures, or your main distribution board, turn off the electricity at the main breaker. If the water is actively pooling around the breaker box itself, do not touch it—wait for a professional.

## 3. Drain the Remaining Water
Once the main water supply is off, there is still water sitting in your pipes. 
*   Turn on all the cold water taps in your home.
*   Flush your toilets. 
This relieves the remaining pressure in the system and minimizes the amount of water that will continue to leak from the burst section.

## 4. Document the Damage
Before you start the massive cleanup effort, take out your phone and record videos and photos of the leak and the resulting damage. This documentation will be crucial when filing an insurance claim.

## 5. Call in the Professionals
A burst pipe is rarely a DIY fix. It requires pipe replacement, pressure testing, and often professional water extraction to prevent mold growth. Don't risk a secondary leak by trying to patch it yourself.
