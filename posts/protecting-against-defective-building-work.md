# What Are Your Rights When Dealing with Defective Building Work?

One of the most stressful experiences a homeowner can face is discovering poor workmanship, structural issues, or defects after paying a contractor. Understanding standard building obligations can save you from a massive financial loss and a lengthy dispute.

## Standard Defect Timelines

If you have recently had major work or a new build completed, legitimate builders are typically bound by specific rectification periods. Industry standards often dictate that:
*   A home builder rectifies minor defects identified by the housing consumer within the first three months of occupation.
*   A home builder rectifies a roof leak identified by the housing consumer within one year from the date of occupation.
*   A home builder rectifies major structural defects identified by the housing consumer within five years from the date of occupation.

## How to Handle a Dispute

If you encounter defects and the artisan is not communicating effectively, you must follow a clear process. Your first step should be to notify the home builder in writing of all complaints requiring attention within a reasonable time. Always keep a copy of all correspondence between yourself and the home builder, and be sure to allow the home builder reasonable access to the property in order to fix the defects.
