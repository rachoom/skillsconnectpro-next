# The Ultimate Winter Home Maintenance Checklist to Avoid Costly Repairs

As the temperatures drop, your home faces a new set of challenges. Cold weather, increased rainfall, and higher electrical loads can stress your property's systems. Taking proactive steps now can save you from shivering through a breakdown and paying premium emergency repair rates later.

Here is your essential winter prep checklist to keep your home warm, dry, and efficient.

## 1. Inspect and Clear Your Gutters
Autumn leaves and debris can clog your gutters and downspouts. When heavy winter rains hit, that blocked water has nowhere to go but over the edge, potentially damaging your foundation, exterior walls, or roof structure. Ensure gutters are completely clear and water flows freely away from your house.

## 2. Check for Drafts and Seal Leaks
A drafty house forces your heating system to work overtime, driving up your electricity bill. 
*   Inspect the weather stripping around your doors and windows.
*   Look for gaps around pipes or cables entering the home.
*   Apply fresh caulk or expanding foam where necessary to keep the cold air out.

## 3. Have Your Roof Inspected
Winter is not the time to discover a roof leak. Check for missing or damaged tiles, cracked flashing around chimneys, and any signs of sagging. If you are uncomfortable getting on a ladder, this is the perfect job to outsource to a local roofing specialist.

## 4. Service Your Geyser and Insulation
Your geyser works hardest during the winter months. Check the pipes leading to and from the geyser for any signs of dripping. Consider wrapping the geyser and exposed hot water pipes in a thermal blanket to reduce heat loss and save electricity.

## 5. Prune Overhanging Tree Branches
High winter winds can cause dead or heavy tree branches to snap and crash into your roof, windows, or power lines. Trim back any branches that pose a threat to your property.
