# The Shocking Water Bill: How to Track Down Hidden Leaks

There is nothing quite like the shock of opening a utility bill that is double or triple its normal amount. If your household's water usage habits haven't changed, a hidden leak is almost certainly the culprit. 

## The Cost of Ignoring Drips

It is easy to ignore minor plumbing issues, but a dripping faucet can waste hundreds of gallons of water a year and inflate your utility bills. A slow drip can quickly turn into an expensive leak, so it is crucial to remain vigilant. 

## Signs You Have a Hidden Leak

If you cannot see a dripping tap, the water is escaping somewhere else. Look out for these indicators:
*   **Unexpected Damp Spots:** If you notice a damp spot that appears randomly on a wall, this is usually an indication that you have a leaking pipe in your ceiling or in your walls.
*   **Mould and Moisture:** Always call in a professional immediately if you spot any signs of moisture damage on your ceilings or walls or notice any mould growth.
*   **The Meter Test:** Turn off all water sources in your home and check your water meter. If the dial is still spinning, you have an active leak.
*   **Low Water Pressure:** A sudden, unexplained drop in water pressure across the house can sometimes indicate a significant break in the main supply line.
